## Adversarial review and decision

Strongest opposing evidence; plausible confounder; simpler alternative; buyer objection; excluded/harmed segment; cost sensitivity; evidence that would reverse the decision. Identify self-review versus independent review. Record each gate result, unresolved risks, owner and next action.
