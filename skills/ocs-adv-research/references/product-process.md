# Customer value, product strategy and process improvement

## Segmentation and ICP

Investopedia describes target markets and demographic, geographic, psychographic and behavioral segmentation. Use these as candidate lenses; validate which variables predict needs and purchase behavior. Avoid demographic stereotypes. An ICP is this skill's operational selection model, not a claim that the source defines a complete B2B framework.

For B2B add firm size, sector, workflow maturity, installed systems, procurement and regulation. For each candidate segment record problem frequency/severity, existing workaround/spend, reachability, alternatives, switching cost, urgency, willingness and ability to pay, and exclusion criteria. Rank with transparent evidence confidence. Choose a primary segment and explicitly defer others. Bottom-up market estimates require reachable accounts, realistic price and adoption assumptions, with ranges; broad market totals do not prove demand.

Map end user, economic buyer, champion, administrator, procurement/security approver and affected non-users. Record each role's incentives, authority, success criterion and veto. User preference does not prove budget authority; buyer enthusiasm does not establish daily usability.

## Paid demand and strategy

Interview about recent behavior: last occurrence, consequences, actual workaround, current spend, approval path and abandoned alternatives. Avoid leading future-intent questions. Recruit representative users, buyers, non-adopters, dissatisfied and churned customers; include disabled participants and people with low digital confidence. Obtain informed consent and record recruitment bias.

Triangulate interviews with observed tasks and behavioral evidence. Evidence strength depends on terms and context: hypothetical interest < credible budget/approval evidence < concrete pilot commitment < paid pilot < sustained purchase and renewal. A refundable reservation or nonbinding letter is weaker than repeated paid use. Record incentive, price, discount, cancellation/refund terms, denominator, follow-through and selection bias. Never treat this ordering as universal proof or a source-derived law.

Test realistic offers and price points using authorized, transparent research. Do not take payment, send outreach, misrepresent product availability or run deceptive purchase flows without authorization. Distinguish willingness to pay from ability to pay and procurement feasibility. State what is untested when transactions cannot be run.

Write a strategy: target segment, job/problem, differentiated promise, alternatives (including doing nothing), distribution/access, pricing hypothesis, constraints and explicit non-goals. Estimate incremental customer value, time-to-value, acquisition, implementation, support and operating costs, contribution margin, payback and retention sensitivity. Give ranges and break-even conditions. Choose the simplest approach that achieves meaningful value within constraints. Elegant architecture receives no demand credit.

## Process optimization

Map the whole journey: inputs, queues, handoffs, rework, exception paths, decision rights and outputs. Measure touch time versus elapsed lead time, completion, first-pass yield, error/rework rate, workload, cost per successful outcome and downstream burden. Identify the bottleneck before automating. Avoid speeding one step by moving work to users or support.

Run a small reversible improvement against a recorded baseline; retain auditability and human exception handling. Use time-series/run charts and suitable control charts to distinguish variation from sustained change. Control limits describe process variation, not customer acceptance limits. Do not claim causal improvement from a simple before/after comparison with concurrent changes. Reassess bottlenecks after each intervention.

## UX/UI and accessibility checklist

- Observe representative critical tasks using realistic devices, content, connectivity and assistive technologies. Test first-use and expert workflows; count unassisted success, time, errors and recovery, with confidence limits where appropriate.
- Review system feedback, familiar language, consistency, user control/undo, error prevention, recognition, efficient paths, uncluttered hierarchy, recovery and help. A heuristic review identifies risks; user testing establishes observed performance.
- Include loading, empty, error, offline, permission, destructive-action and recovery states. Use readable content, clear navigation, predictable controls and responsive layouts. Avoid dark patterns and forced complexity.
- Use WCAG 2.2 AA as a default web acceptance target unless requirements specify otherwise. Evaluate all applicable A and AA criteria across full pages and complete processes. Combine automated tools with keyboard, screen-reader, focus, zoom/reflow, contrast, input/error and target-size checks. Include accessible authentication and focus not obscured. A checklist or automated scan alone cannot establish conformance.
- Record defects, severity, affected segment, task impact, reproduction and retest. Protect minority access needs from being hidden by overall averages.

## Lifecycle and brand trust

Define activation as first verified customer value, not registration. Track time-to-value and completion funnel by cohort/segment. Measure retention over the natural usage interval and state who is eligible to return. Separate customer/logo churn from revenue churn and voluntary from involuntary churn. Investigate reasons with consented exit research and support data.

Measure CSAT with a stated question, scale, trigger, positive-response rule, response rate and distribution. Pair sentiment with task success, support burden and observed retention; survey responders may be unrepresentative. Measure actual qualified referrals and referred-customer activation/retention separately from recommendation intent. Investigate trust through complaints, reliability, clear promises and recovery. Neither NPS nor CSAT alone proves loyalty, market fit or causal revenue impact. Never optimize engagement through manipulation or impair cancellation/data export.
